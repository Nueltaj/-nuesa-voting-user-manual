# Introduction

This guide walks you through the process of casting your vote in the NUESA virtual election. The election is conducted entirely online, ensuring every eligible student can participate securely from wherever they are.

The system is designed to guarantee:
- One vote per student
- Secure and private login
- Automatic and accurate vote counting
- Zero tolerance for duplicate voting
- Free and transparent results

# Section 1: Voter Registration 
Before you can vote, you must register through the official NUESA Google Form.
Provide the following information accurately: 
- Full Name
- Matric Number
- Department
- Level
- Email Address
- Phone Number 

Once you have filled out all fields, click the Submit button at the bottom of the page. Your information will then be reviewed by the Electoral Committee to confirm your eligibility.

# Section 2: Your Voting Credentials 
Once your eligibility has been confirmed, you will receive an email from the official NUESA account containing the following: 
- Your Full Name
- Voting Website Link
- Matric Number
- Unique Voting Code
-  Election Date and Time
- Voting Instructions 

**Keep your Unique Voting Code private. It is tied exclusively to your identity and can only be used once.**

# Section 3: Logging In 
Open the Voting Website Link provided in your email. You will be directed to the _login page_ of the voting application, as shown below.

![Login Page](<login.png>) 
> Figure 1: Voter Authentication — Login Page

Enter your **Matric Number** (e.g. ENG/2021/001) and your **Unique Voting Code** in the provided fields, then click the **Access Voting Portal** button to proceed.

# Section 4: Casting Your Vote 
You will be directed to the voting dashboard, where all available positions are listed. A progress bar at the top tracks how many positions you have voted for.

![Voting Dashboard](<voting_dashboard.png>)
> Figure 2: Voting Dashboard — Cast Your Vote

For each position, the names of the candidates are displayed along with their department. You may click the “Manifesto” button beside a candidate’s name to view their profile before making your choice. Select your preferred candidate by clicking the radio button next to their name. 

You are required to vote for all five positions — President, Vice President, Secretary, Financial Secretary, and Public Relations Officer — before your submission will be
accepted.

![Position Voting Dashboard](<Position_Voting_Dashboard.png>)
> Figure 3: All Positions Voted — Confirm Selections Active

Once all positions have been voted for, the status bar at the bottom will display “All positions voted. Confirm your selections first.” The Confirm Selections button will
become active. 

![Selections Confirmed](<Selections_Confirmed.png>)
> Figure 4: Selections Confirmed — Submit Your Final Vote

After clicking **Confirm Selections**, the bar will update to “Selections confirmed. Submit your final vote.” Click the **Submit** button to cast your vote. This action is final and cannot be undone.

# Section 5: After Voting 
After submitting your final vote, the screen below will appear, confirming that your vote has been encrypted and securely recorded on the NUESA election server.

![Vote Recorded](<vote_recorded.png>)
> Figure 5: Vote Recorded Successfully


The confirmation screen also shows the following automatic actions: 
- Voting Code Expired — your one-time voting code has been invalidated
- Session Closed — your voting session has been terminated automatically
- Vote Anonymised — your identity has been decoupled from your ballot.  
  
You may then click **Return to Home** or **Logout Securely** to exit the application.

# Section 6: Troubleshooting and FAQs 
## Registration 
**What if I accidentally submitted the form twice?**  
Do not worry. Duplicate submissions are automatically detected and removed. Only one entry per student will be retained.

**What if I made an error in my matric number or details?**  
Contact the support team as soon as possible with your correct information so your record can be updated before verification is completed. 

**How do I know if my registration was successful?**  
You will receive a confirmation email once your registration has been reviewed and your eligibility confirmed. If you do not receive any email closer to the election date, contact the support team.

## Voting Credentials 
**What if I did not receive my voting email?**   
First, check that you are looking in the correct email inbox, including your spam or junk folder. If the email is still not found, contact the support team for assistance.

**What if my voting code does not work?**  
Ensure you are entering the exact code provided in your email, with no extra spaces.
If the problem persists, contact the support team. 

**Can I share my voting code with someone else?**  
No. Your voting code is strictly personal and tied to your identity. Sharing it compromises your anonymous status and violates the election rules.

**What if I lost or deleted the voting email?**  
You may request a resend from the support team. Your original voting code will remain valid until it is used. 

## Login 
**What if I cannot access the voting website link?**  
Ensure you have a stable internet connection and that the election period is currently active. If the issue persists, contact the support team.

**What if the system says my matric number is not recognized?**  
This may mean your registration was not successfully verified. Contact the support team immediately. 

**What if I enter my voting code incorrectly multiple times?**  
This will not prevent you from eventually casting your vote. Carefully re-enter the exact code from your email and try again.

## During Voting 
**What if the page refreshes or I lose internet connection while voting?**  
Your progress is temporarily saved on the server. Log back in using your credentials to continue. Note that if your vote was already submitted before the disconnection, it will have been recorded. 

**Can I change my vote after confirming my selections?**  
No. Once your final vote is submitted, it cannot be edited or reversed. Review your selections carefully before clicking Confirm Selections.  

**What if a position has no candidate listed?**   
Positions with no registered candidates will not appear on your voting dashboard.   

## After Voting 
**How do I know my vote was counted?**  
A confirmation screen will appear immediately after submission showing “Vote Recorded Successfully”, indicating your vote has been encrypted and recorded on the NUESA election server. 

**Can I vote again if I feel I made the wrong choice?**  
No. The system allows only one submission per student. 
This is to ensure the integrity of the election.  

**When will results be announced?**  
Results will be announced at a date and time communicated by the Electoral Committee. Keep an eye on official NUESA channels for updates. 

## General 
**Is my vote truly anonymous?**  
Yes. Once your vote is submitted, your identity is automatically decoupled from your ballot. Only the vote itself is retained — not your identity.

**Who do I contact if I have a technical problem?**  
Reach out to the NUESA support team through the official contact channel provided
in Section 7 of this guide.
